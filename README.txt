PAGES
- index.html: home page, featured products, interactive tabs
- about.html: story, mission and vision
- menu.html: searchable/filterable products and demo cart
- services.html: cakes, delivery, eat-in information and FAQs
- gallery.html: image gallery with lightbox
- enquiry.html: validated enquiry form with simulated AJAX-style response
- contact.html: validated contact form and map
