About Mhlongo Dzonga Bakery

From our family oven in Tzaneen to your home.

Our story

Started in 2020 in Tzaneen, Limpopo, Mhlongo Dzonga Fresh Bakery grew from one oven into a bakery serving the community. We value fresh baking, local ingredients and the warmth of family recipes.

From farmhouse bread to cakes for special occasions, our goal is to make every visit feel welcoming.

Our mission

To bake fresh, affordable and delicious products using local Limpopo ingredients, while supporting jobs and serving our community with care and honesty.

Our vision

To become one of Limpopo's most loved bakeries, known for freshness, quality and community spirit.
